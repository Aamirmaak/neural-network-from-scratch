## Anti-Fabrication Checklist

Before recording any result:

- [ ] Was the experiment actually run?
- [ ] Are these actual measurements?
- [ ] Is the configuration documented?
- [ ] Is the random seed documented?
- [ ] Can this be reproduced?
- [ ] Are failures documented honestly?

**If any answer is NO, do not record the result.**
